Frequently asked questions

Where is my team saved? On your iPhone. NextIn has no account or cloud sync.

How do I delete my data? Delete a player or reset playing time in the app, or uninstall the app to remove its local data from the device. iPhone backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

How do substitution reminders work? Allow notifications for NextIn in iOS Settings. You can change or turn off reminders in the app.
