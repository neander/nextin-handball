Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Data in the app

You can enter player names, jersey numbers, lineups, availability, and playing time. NextIn stores this information locally on your iPhone so the app can work. It is not sent to us, and NextIn does not sync it to a server.

Notifications and Live Activity

If you allow notifications, NextIn uses iOS to schedule local substitution reminders. A reminder or Live Activity may show player names and the next substitution on the lock screen. You can change notification and Live Activity settings in iOS.

Tracking and third parties

NextIn contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking and does not share team data from the app with third parties. App Store purchases and payments are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Storage and deletion

Team data remains on the device until you change or delete it. You can delete individual players and reset playing time in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device. iOS backups may contain app data depending on your Apple settings.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it to answer your question and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed for support. You can request deletion by email.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions: nextin@nndr.net.
